About Initiative

The Gap

Ghana and Cote d’Ivoire produce over 60% of the world’s cocoa, yet capture only 2–4% of the value. The highest margins are earned in processing, branding, and distribution outside Africa.

The Opportunity

Beginning with the 2026/27 crop season, Ghana has directed that at least 50% of cocoa beans be processed locally — creating a timely opening for investment into processing infrastructure and market development.

The Diaspora as the 17th Region

Ghana’s 17th Region concept treats the global diaspora as a formal partner in investment, innovation, skills transfer, and long-term nation building.

Join Us in Building Ghana’s Cocoa Future

The Chocolate Future offers a credible platform for investment that is economically strategic, socially meaningful, and nationally aligned — with Ghana as our home.

How It Works

Connect Capital

We channel diaspora investment directly into Ghana’s cocoa-processing industry, aligning with national policy objectives for local value addition.

Support Processing Capacity

Funding flows to processing infrastructure, working capital, and market development that help transform Ghana from raw-bean exporter to stronger participant in the higher-value segments of the cocoa economy.

Create Employment & Skills Transfer

Every investment creates jobs, encourages skills transfer, and contributes to a model of development that aligns commercial returns with national transformation.
